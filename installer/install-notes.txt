GlassPlayer community fork by N3moStudio
Original application and glass interface by Jonzu00.

This installer adds GlassPlayer to your Start menu and Windows Installed apps.
It installs for your Windows account without administrator access.
An optional desktop shortcut is available on the next page.

SoundCloud, YouTube Music and local audio play inside GlassPlayer. Spotify
playlist entries use carefully matched YouTube audio; the Spotify desktop app
does not need to be open. Some recordings may not have a playable match.

To use your library, open Settings, choose Connect library and sign in to your
own Spotify account. Then choose Spotify > Sync playlists. This saves playlist
names, covers and song lists on this PC. Sync again to refresh Spotify changes.
Sync does not change your Spotify playlists or connect other people's accounts.
The experimental sign-in connection requires Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime.

No Spotify Client ID, account session, or personal configuration is included.
This is an independent GPL-3.0 community fork, not an official Spotify product.
